# I See Fish

Paste a decklist and watch it goldfish. It uses your own card images from **ScryMirror**, plus exact draw math and a Monte Carlo simulation.

## Use it on your phone or iPad (hosted version)

The `goldfish/static` folder is a complete web app that needs no server. In this mode it loads card data and images live from [Scryfall](https://scryfall.com) in your browser, remembers cards for 7 days, and can be installed to your Home Screen.

**Publish it with GitHub Pages (free, about 2 minutes):**
1. In the repo on GitHub: **Settings -> Pages -> Build and deployment -> Source: GitHub Actions**.
2. Go to the **Actions** tab, open **Deploy to GitHub Pages**, and wait for the green check (or click **Run workflow**).
3. Your app is at `https://<your-github-name>.github.io/Goldfish-/`. Open it on your phone.

GitHub Pages is free for public repositories. If the repo is private, use any free static host instead (for example drag the `goldfish/static` folder onto <https://app.netlify.com/drop>).

**Install it as an app:**
- **iPhone / iPad (Safari):** Share button -> **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** menu -> **Install app** / **Add to Home screen**.

**Touch controls:** tap any card on the table and its menu opens as a sheet at the bottom of the screen: the main action (Cast, Play land, Tap or Untap) comes first, then View card, counters, sacrifice and moving between zones. Tap outside the sheet or **Back** to close it; nothing happens to the card. Elsewhere, tapping a card opens the card viewer.

**Phones and iPads:** the Goldfish Table fits on one screen, Arena style. Battlefield rows share the height and the cards size themselves to fit, your hand sits along the bottom, and the library, command zone, graveyard and exile are piles beside the battlefield like a home game (tap one to see its cards). The buttons become one row of icons (🎲 new game, ▶ autopilot turn, ⏩ autopilot to turn, ⟳ next turn, ＋ token, ↶ undo, 📜 log, 🎨 playmat, ⚡ fast), and 📜 opens the game log as a drawer.

**Card viewer:** swipe the card (or use the ‹ › buttons or arrow keys) to move to the next or previous card around it, pick another **printing** (with prices) and choose **Use this printing in my deck** to switch the art for this visit, and shop for it at **TCGplayer, Card Kingdom or ManaPool**. The shop links are plain links for now; affiliate parameters can be added in `AFFILIATE` near the top of the viewer code in `app.js`. The viewer also shows how the engine reads the card.

**Same Wi-Fi only, using your PC's ScryMirror images:** double-click `goldfish\start-lan.bat`, then open the address it prints on your phone.

## Run it on your PC with your own ScryMirror (Windows)

1. Install Python 3 if you don't have it (python.org, tick "Add to PATH"). It needs nothing else.
2. Double-click `start.bat` (in the top folder, or the one inside `goldfish`). Keep the whole downloaded folder together; the launcher needs `server.py` and `static` beside it. Your browser opens at <http://127.0.0.1:8765>.

It reads `%USERPROFILE%\Documents\ScryMirror` by default:

- `data\scryfall.sqlite` for card data (Oracle text, mana cost, types, printings)
- `data\images\normal\<set>\<cn>_en[_<face>].jpg` for the card images

Different location: `start.bat --scrymirror "D:\path\to\ScryMirror"` (or `python server.py --scrymirror ...`).
Everything runs locally and only reads your files; nothing is uploaded.

## The five tabs

| Tab | What you see |
|---|---|
| **1 · Deck** | Load/save decklists. A **Tags** box gives every card tags for its roles, triggers, effects, abilities, keywords and creature types: search them (`role:ramp -type:land`, `trigger:etb tribe:merfolk`), save a result as your own tag (`my:combo`), or send it to Draw Odds. A **Strengths and weaknesses** box names what the deck does well and badly (ramp, card draw, removal, wipes, counters, protection, tutors, finishers, land count, color sources, and themes like spellslinger or graveyard) using common deckbuilding rules of thumb. A *curve wall* of your actual cards stacked by mana value, piles of lands with the colors they make, and checks for card count, color identity, legality and missing images. |
| **2 · Opening Hands** | Fanned 7-card draws with London mulligans (the bottom picks are greyed out), 12 sample hands at once, and the exact odds of each land count in your opening 7. |
| **3 · Goldfish Table** | A playable table. Click cards to play them (mana auto-taps from the right lands), click permanents to tap them, and right-click to move cards. Or let the autopilot play turns with animated draws, land drops, casts and attacks. Includes undo. |
| **4 · Simulation** | Thousands of autopiloted games: mana by turn (median plus a 10–90% band), land-drop odds, commander cast turn, goldfish kill turn, and a heatmap of when every card gets played. You can replay a rough, typical or nut-draw game turn by turn with card images, then send it to the table. A **Suggestions** box reads the run and points out missed land drops, flood, low keep rates, a late commander, cards that are drawn but never cast, and how much of the deck isn't simulated yet. The **mirror match** plays your deck against itself or a saved deck, lists the cards linked to winning or losing that matchup, shows what your fastest wins and fastest losses have in common, and finds pairs of cards that do much better or worse together. |
| **5 · Draw Odds** | Click any group of cards and get the exact hypergeometric odds of seeing at least *k* of them by each turn. |

## What the engine understands

The engine reads each card's Oracle text and turns it into abilities. The **Deck** tab shows every line of rules text, colored by whether it's *simulated*, *doesn't matter vs. a goldfish*, *approximated* or *not simulated*, so you always know what the numbers include.

- **Turn structure:** untap, upkeep, draw, main phase (land, spells, planeswalkers, equip, activated abilities), beginning of combat, attacks, combat damage, end step, and cleanup of "until end of turn" effects.
- **Mana:** colored costs, hybrid, Phyrexian, X, taplands and check lands, fetches, MDFC land backs, rocks and dorks (with summoning sickness), Treasure, rituals and triggered mana, cost reducers ("Creature spells you cast cost {1} less"), extra land drops (Exploration, Burgeoning-style "put a land from your hand"), and commander tax.
- **Triggers:** enters the battlefield, attacks, Landfall, "whenever you cast a (noncreature / instant or sorcery / creature…) spell", "whenever another creature / Elf / artifact enters", upkeep, beginning of combat, end step, combat damage to a player, and "whenever you draw".
- **Effects:**
  - creature tokens with power/toughness, colors and keywords, using the real token art from ScryMirror
  - **token replacement effects:** Doubling Season, Parallel Lives, Mondrak and Elspeth, Storm Slayer (they stack), Divine Visitation (creature tokens become Angels), Chatterfang and Peregrin Took (extra Squirrels or a Food), Academy Manufactor and Bilbo (Clue, Food and Treasure). Order used: conversions, then additions, then doublers. Doubling Season also doubles counters and starting/+ loyalty.
  - **token triggers:** "whenever a token enters / you create a token / a token leaves", including Belladonna Took's first-second-third-time-this-turn ability, Rosie Cotton, Mirkwood Bats, Nadier's Nightblade, Gyome, and Rapacious Guest's combat Food
  - **Food economy:** spends spare mana and Foods on "sacrifice a Food" abilities (Greta's cards and counters, Savvy Hunter and Peregrin Took's draws), with the payoffs: Nuka-Cola's Treasure, Rapacious Guest's counters, Mirkwood Bats and Nadier's Nightblade. Cabbage Merchant turns pairs of Foods into mana.
  - **Spare Foods:** Foods are spent in this order: draw cards while your hand is short of seven (Greta, Savvy Hunter, Peregrin Took), then return useful cards (Samwise returns historic cards as often as the Foods allow, Cauldron Familiar comes back to the battlefield), then counters. Foods that Frodo or Sorin still need to crack for their 3 life are kept back.
  - **Life and combos:** tracks your life total; "whenever you gain life" and "whenever an opponent loses life" triggers; lifelink; Foods cracked for life when something cares (Sam makes it {1}). Exquisite Blood + Sanguine Bond is recognized as an infinite loop and kills the goldfish.
  - **Extort and transforming cards:** Extort is paid from spare mana after you finish casting ("whenever you cast a spell, pay {W/B}: each opponent loses 1 life and you gain that much"), so it never stops a spell from being cast, and it feeds Exquisite Blood and Sanguine Bond. Sorin of House Markov has a postcombat main phase: if you gained 3 or more life that turn (the engine cracks a Food for it) he transforms into Sorin, Ravenous Neonate with his real loyalty (Doubling Season doubles it), shows the planeswalker art, and uses +2 (Food) or -1 (damage equal to the life gained) at once. The -6 needs an opponent's creature, so it is tagged as such. The back face's rules lines are listed on the Deck tab too.
  - **Liliana the Faultless:** "whenever another creature or planeswalker you control enters, you gain 1 life" fires for every creature and token (so Doubling Season and token makers gain a lot of life), and for a planeswalker such as Sorin returning transformed. The life counts toward Frodo's and Sorin's 3-life conditions and triggers Sanguine Bond.
  - **Sagas:** every Saga (238 filed with `node tools/fetch-sets.js --query "t:saga"`) is played chapter by chapter: chapter I when it enters, the next chapter after each of your draw steps, sacrificed after the last one. Chapters that list two numbers ("I, II") fire on both turns, modal chapters pick the best mode, Sagas that end by transforming become their creature side (Fable of the Mirror-Breaker), and Praetors and Dominants pay to transform into their Saga and come back as the creature after the last chapter. 182 Sagas are read in full; 58 chapter or back-face lines are still ignored (cards exiled with the Saga, time travel, bending, Doctor Who and Un-set one-offs). Approximations: chapters that only touch opponents' hands, libraries or attack choices do nothing; "whenever you cast a spell this turn" chapters resolve once; The Mending of Dominaria does not shuffle the graveyard back; read ahead always starts at chapter I; transform conditions on Dominants ("only if you control...") are not checked.
  - **Five-color commanders:** Najeela makes a tapped, attacking Warrior for each attacking Warrior and pays {W}{U}{B}{R}{G} for another combat with trample, lifelink and haste (approximation: taken after combat, at most twice a turn, and it untaps every creature rather than only the attackers). Sisay counts colors among your other legendary permanents and fetches the best legendary permanent below her power. Derevi comes from the command zone for {1}{G}{W}{U} whatever the tax. Zur fetches an enchantment with mana value 3 or less when it attacks. Relic of Legends lets legendary creatures tap for mana, summoning sick or not.
  - **Stax and wheel pieces:** Tangle Wire enters with four fade counters, loses one each upkeep, taps that many of the active player's permanents (your own Wire first) and fades away; fading works for any card. Trinisphere makes every spell cost at least three while it is untapped. Teferi's Puzzle Box sends each player's hand to the bottom in their draw step and redraws it, so draw punishers (Nekusar, Underworld Dreams) fire for every card the goldfish redraws. Sulfuric Vortex deals 2 to each player in their upkeep and stops life gain. Pestilence turns spare mana into damage to each creature and each player after combat, stopping before it kills your own nontoken creatures or takes you below 10 life. Miracle: a miracle card drawn as the first card of your turn is cast for its miracle cost when you can pay (a wheel only with four or fewer other cards in hand or a draw/discard punisher out; a sweeper only when you have no creatures). Molten Psyche shuffles each hand away, redraws that many, and with three artifacts deals the cards the opponent drew this turn. Approximations: Tangle Wire and Trinisphere are not cast against a goldfish (like the other symmetrical locks); Pestilence's "sacrifice if no creatures" clause is not applied; Mindslaver does nothing; Trinisphere ignores X.
  - **Graveyard, sacrifice and sweepers:** sacrificed or destroyed creatures now go to the graveyard (a commander to the command zone), and "when ~ leaves the battlefield" fires with the creature's power (Rapacious Guest drains). Cauldron Familiar comes back for a Food (and drains again). Samwise's three Foods return the best historic card (artifact, legend or Saga) from the graveyard, once a turn. Ashnod's Altar sacrifices fodder (tokens first, Cauldron Familiar when you have a Food) for {C}{C} each, but only to cast a card that matters: a combo piece, a token or counter doubler, or your commander. Dusk // Dawn: Dawn is cast from the graveyard (and exiled) when it would return at least 2 creature cards with power 2 or less; Dusk is only cast to set that up and never over your own power 3+ creatures; Ring loots prefer to discard it.
  - **Planeswalker and land extras:** Grist's +1 mills a card into your graveyard and his -5 drains the goldfish for the creature cards there (used as a finisher when it kills, or when the graveyard is big). Restless Cottage becomes a 4/4 and attacks (making its Food) when you have {2}{B}{G} to spare without tapping it, on turns after it entered. Shire Terrace is sacrificed for a basic land only when that adds a color you are missing. Jennifer Walters transforms into The Sensational She-Hulk (6/6 reach, trample) when you have {3}{G}{W}{W} spare. Hazel's Brewmaster's last line needs creature cards in an opponent's graveyard, and She-Hulk's damage reflection needs something to damage your creatures, so both are tagged "Needs opponent".
  - **Enlightened Confidant and modal double-faced creatures:** at your end step, after gaining life, Confidant surveils: if the top card's mana value is no more than the life you gained it goes to your hand, otherwise it stays on top. Jennifer Walters can be cast as The Sensational She-Hulk for {3}{G}{W}{W} when that is the best use of your mana.
  - **Scry:** scry sends surplus lands (once you have about 6) and cards far too expensive to cast to the bottom, and never a combo piece, a token doubler or your commander. Viscera Seer sacrifices a token to scry only when you have 8 or more tokens and the top card is one you would bottom. Path of Ancestry scrys when you cast a creature that shares a type with your commander (approximated: it does not track which mana paid for the spell, so it is flagged on the Deck tab).
  - **Spellslinger and combo decks (tested on a Veyran deck):** Magecraft and "whenever you cast or copy" triggers, Niv-Mizzet's "whenever a player casts" and "whenever you draw" (every draw, including from effects), Rhystic Study (the other player pays {1} when they can), Veyran's extra trigger on instants and sorceries, and Curiosity (it goes on the creature that pings, so Niv-Mizzet + Curiosity is recognised as the infinite loop it is). X spells put all your spare mana into X (Fireball, Comet Storm, Walking Ballista, which can also shoot its counters for the last points of damage). Extra turns (Time Warp) are played. Card selection: Dig Through Time, Fact or Fiction (the best three, approximated), Expressive Iteration (approximated), Ponder, Brainstorm, Frantic Search (discard and untap lands). Copying: Thousand-Year Storm, and Expansion // Explosion (cast in response to your own cheap spell). Snapcaster Mage flashes back the best spell in your graveyard. Anger of the Gods and other "damage to each creature" sweepers, Dramatic Reversal, Mind Stone cashing in, bounce lands, and storm-style counts. In a mirror match counterspells can also be free (Fierce Guardianship with a commander out, Mindbreak Trap), pitched (Force of Will, Force of Negation), return the spell (Remand), give mana (Mana Drain), give the other player a token (Swan Song, An Offer You Can't Refuse) or be a Pact (only cast with 5 mana to pay for it).
  - **Death, sacrifice and combo engines (tested on the "Second Breakfast" Frodo + Sam list):** "whenever a creature dies" triggers (Blood Artist, Zulaport Cutthroat, Grave Pact, Skullclamp) fire for every creature that dies: sacrificed, destroyed or in combat, tokens included; Persist (Kitchen Finks) returns the creature with a -1/-1 counter. After combat, a free sacrifice outlet (Viscera Seer, Ashnod's Altar) cashes in your tokens when two or more death triggers are out, you have a wide board, or it is lethal. Skullclamp is put on 1-toughness tokens for {1} to draw two each. Sheoldred and Smothering Tithe punish the other player's draws (mirror matches), your own Sheoldred gains 2 life per draw, Felidar Sovereign wins at your upkeep with 40 life, Aetherflux Reservoir pays 50 life for the win, Necropotence replaces your draw with paying life at the end step, Sylvan Library keeps the best of three cards, Toxic Deluge (mirror matches) picks X to take more of their board than yours, Mox Diamond discards a spare land, Urborg makes every land tap for {B}, Sakura-Tribe Elder is sacrificed for a land after combat, Fauna Shaman trades a weak creature for a better one, Deathrite Shaman exiles graveyard cards for life or drain, The One Ring draws one more card each turn at the cost of life, Heliod is a creature only with 5 devotion to white, and fetch lands really crack (below).
  - **Fetch lands:** a fetch land is cracked as soon as it is played: you pay the life, it goes to the graveyard (so it counts for Deathrite Shaman and similar), and the best land of the right types comes out of the library, followed by a shuffle. Landfall triggers twice. "Best" means the land that makes the colours your hand and commander need and you do not have yet, a dual over a basic, and an untapped land (a shock land is fetched untapped for 2 life only while your life is above 12). Types are respected (Marsh Flats finds Plains or Swamp cards, so Godless Shrine, Scrubland and Bayou, but never a plain Forest). A fetch land with nothing left to fetch is not played.
  - **Planeswalker decks (tested on a Loyalty Program list led by Atraxa):** planeswalkers can activate more than once: Oath of Teferi (twice a turn), The Chain Veil ({4}, {T} for another round), Teferi, Master of Time (twice a round), and Carth the Lion adds a loyalty counter to every activation. Proliferate is a real effect on loyalty and counters (Atraxa's end step, Contagion Engine and Karn's Bastion with {4}, Flux Channeler, Inexorable Tide, Evolution Sage, Thrummingbird). "Look at the top cards and take one" works as written: Vivien Reid, Narset, Ajani, Carth, and Atraxa, Grand Unifier (one card of each type), plus Eternal Witness, Tamiyo and Liliana, the Last Hope bringing things back from the graveyard, and Wrenn's mill. Oko turns a Food into a 3/3 Elk (or, in a mirror match, their best creature), Nissa and Wrenn turn lands into creatures and change how lands make mana, Karn animates an artifact, Kaya's ghostform brings a creature back with a Spirit, Spark Double copies your best planeswalker or enters-the-battlefield creature, Oath of Teferi flickers, Teferi tucks or bounces a permanent, Vraska turns a creature into a Treasure, Liliana of the Veil makes everyone discard, Contagion Engine shrinks only the other player's creatures, pain lands (City of Brass, Mana Confluence, Ancient Tomb) cost life, and Mystic Remora pays its cumulative upkeep only against a real opponent.
  - **Gollum:** once Gollum has dealt combat damage (he has skulk), his end-step trigger drains the goldfish for all the life you gained that turn (Extort, Liliana, Foods, lifelink).
  - **Tutors:** "search your library for a card" (Vampiric Tutor to the top, Demonic Tutor and Diabolic Intent to your hand, typed ones like "a creature card"). It fetches, in order: the missing half of a combo, the cheaper half of a combo you have neither of, a token doubler, a cheap mana rock if you are short on mana, then the strongest card you can afford. It never fetches a second copy of a legend already in play. A tutor that completes a combo is cast first. Diabolic Intent needs a token or a plain creature to sacrifice.
  - **Counters:** one engine for every kind: +1/+1 and -1/-1 cancel, Doubling Season doubles them, loyalty and named kinds (indestructible, shield...) are tracked, proliferate adds one of each, "that many" amounts (Treebeard), typed targets ("target Halfling or Treefolk", "each Soldier you control"), and "whenever +1/+1 counters are put on a creature" triggers.
  - **The Ring:** "the Ring tempts you" counts temptations and chooses a Ring-bearer (a creature that cares, like Frodo, first; otherwise the strongest). The Ring's four abilities unlock in order: loot when the bearer attacks (level 2), and each opponent loses 3 life when it deals combat damage (level 4). The Ring panel, the bearer's 💍 highlight and a banner update every time it tempts. Right-click a creature to tempt by hand. Boromir's "sacrifice: the Ring tempts you" is used only when that temptation unlocks something (the loot at 2, the drain at 4, or Frodo becoming a bearer who draws) and another creature can carry the Ring. Frodo's conditions are respected: he needs 3 life gained that turn, and draws only while he is the bearer after two temptations; the engine cracks a Food for the life he needs.
  - **Lands:** check lands, slow and fast lands, shock lands, reveal lands (Snarls), Canopy Vista and The Shire all check the board (or your hand) when they enter, instead of always entering untapped.
  - **Also:** Faeburrow Elder (a mana per color you control), Lotho's second-spell Treasure, and planeswalker ultimates that make "creatures get +2/+2" emblems (used only when your board is wide)
  - Treasure, Clue and Food tokens; Clues get cracked with spare mana
  - card draw
  - burn and drain to the face
  - +1/+1 counters
  - team pumps like Overrun and Craterhoof (+X/+X)
  - land ramp
  - extra combats, untaps and proliferate
  - "for each creature / land you control" counts
- **Permanents:** anthems (including tribal ones like "Other Elves you control get +1/+1"), keyword grants, Equipment (auto-equipped to your best attacker), Auras, and planeswalkers (activates its best loyalty ability each turn).
- **Combat:** attacks with everything that can, including haste, double strike, vigilance and defender. Tracks 21 commander damage as a win in Commander.
- **Mulligans:** London mulligan with a free first mulligan in Commander and a keep range you set.

**Playmat & sleeves:** the 🎨 button on the Goldfish Table and Opening Hands tabs picks a playmat (or your own image) and a sleeve colour. It is saved in your browser. Card images are never covered: sleeves only colour the card edge and the card backs. To add a design, add an entry to the list at the top of `goldfish/static/looks.js` (it can take an image file and a shop link for a licensed partner).

**Manual tools** on the Goldfish Table handle anything the engine doesn't:
- **+ Token** creates any token, including the ones your deck makes.
- Right-click a card to add or remove +1/+1 counters, change loyalty, sacrifice it, toggle summoning sickness, attach it, or move it between zones.
- Click the opponent's life to deal damage.
- Undo works on all of it.

**Still not simulated** (flagged in red on the Deck tab):
- "dies" triggers (sacrifice and leave-the-battlefield triggers are simulated)
- copying spells
- stack interaction
- complex modal or "choose one" choices
- extra turns
- anything that needs an opponent's board

The goldfish never blocks, never removes anything, and never interacts.

## Policy lab: learning by trial and error (beta)

Some choices have no right answer you can read off a card: should a fetch land be cracked at once, or held? The **Policy lab** on the Simulation tab settles it with evidence. It plays the same games (same shuffles, same seats) once with each option, in goldfish mode (how early you win) or as a mirror match (win rate), and shows each option's score with its margin of error and whether it beat the baseline by more than chance. An option only counts as better when it wins by more than chance would explain (about 95% sure); otherwise the answer is "no clear difference", which is a real answer. **Use this** saves the choice for that deck in your browser, and the autopilot, the simulation and mirror matches then use it. **Reset** goes back to the defaults. The lab's choices describe what cards do, never card names ("sacrificing tokens to 'whenever a creature dies' payoffs", "paying life for cards"), so any card that works the same way is covered. **It only offers the choices this deck can use:** each one needs at least one card that works that way (a sacrifice outlet plus a death payoff plus a token maker, a counterspell, a land that searches for a land...), and the lab says which cards brought it in. The rest are hidden, with a note saying what they would need. The lab also tests **which creature type "choose a creature type" cards name** (Kindred Discovery, Cavern of Souls, Vanquisher's Banner, Herald's Horn, Steely Resolve and friends): the deck's detected tribe is the baseline, and the next best types are the challengers. Besides that, it tests fetch land timing (crack at once, hold when a shuffle helps, hold until needed, hold until the end of the turn); the engine's `opts.policy` and `comparePolicies` are built so more choices can be added the same way.

What it found so far (thousands of games on the Second Breakfast, Veyran and old Frodo lists, checked again on fresh shuffles):
- **Holding mana open for counterspells loses.** Playing counterspells only with spare mana won 7 to 11 points more often in every Veyran matchup (z between 4.7 and 7.9), so that is now the default (`holdCounters = no`). The old behaviour is still available in the lab.
- **Fetch lands:** cracking at once is as good as or slightly better than every way of holding, so the default stays.
- **Opening hands depend on the deck:** keeping 3 to 5 lands won 0.8 points more often for the old Frodo list (z 4.7), while for Veyran the default keeps are best (3 or 4 lands loses 1.7 points, z -6.9). That is why the lab saves its result per deck instead of changing one global rule.
- **No measurable effect:** Necropotence's hand size, and when to sacrifice tokens to Blood Artist (never sacrificing is a hair worse in a goldfish, z -2.7).
- **Close:** being more aggressive when they could kill you next turn (attack caution 0.5) won 0.6 to 0.8 points more often in two of three matchups (z 2.1 each). That is about what chance produces when many options are compared, so it is not adopted; more games would settle it.

## Mirror match (beta)

On the **Simulation** tab, the **Mirror match** card plays your deck against itself or against another saved deck, instead of against a goldfish that never fights back. Two full games run side by side and are linked: damage, drain and lifelink move both life totals, creatures block each other (flying, reach, menace, first strike, double strike, deathtouch, trample and lifelink are handled), creatures that lose a fight go to the graveyard, and the first player to reach 0 life or deal 21 commander damage wins. Seats alternate (you are on the play in every other match), so playing a deck against a copy of itself comes out even. You get win rates with a 95% range, the average length, win rate on the play and on the draw, how often matches end by life or by commander damage, a histogram of the turn each side wins on, and a turn-by-turn replay of a quick, a typical and a long match.

**Interaction in a mirror match:** removal and counterspells now work. Cards that "destroy" or "exile target" creature, permanent, artifact, enchantment or planeswalker hit the other player's best target (not a 1/1 token, not hexproof, and not an indestructible one for "destroy"); Beast Within, Generous Gift and Pongify give them their token; Swords gives them life; Path to Exile and Assassin's Trophy fetch them a land; bounce sends the best creature back; burn goes at a creature it kills when the face is not at risk; edicts, and sweepers (Dusk, The Battle of Bywater, Wrath effects) are cast only when they cost the other side more than yours. Counterspells are held: with one in hand the autopilot keeps that much mana open on its own turn, then counters the other player's spell if it is worth it (a commander, planeswalker, combo piece, token doubler or a spell costing 3 or more; "noncreature" and "unless its controller pays" are respected). On the Deck tab these lines show "Mirror matches only", since they still do nothing against a goldfish.

**What it does not do yet:** instant-speed removal on the other player's turn (removal is used on your own turn), tapping or freezing creatures, protection and ward, "choose one" modes, copying spells, and cards that need the other player's hand or library. The autopilot only has simple rules for when to attack, block, hold mana and what to counter (it will not bluff or plan around cards it cannot see). Treat the numbers as a comparison between two builds, not as a prediction of a real game. Normal goldfish results are unchanged.

The Zul Ashur list adds reanimation (Reanimate, Animate Dead, Necromancy, Dread Return, Victimize), Entomb, Bloodghast, Gravecrawler, Reassembling Skeleton, Relentless Dead, Zombie lords, Snuff Out's life cost, Everflowing Chalice kicker and Cabal Coffers. The Death Tax list adds Teysa Karlov (a dying creature's triggers trigger twice, tokens get vigilance and lifelink), Cruel Celebrant, Luminous Broodmoth, Reveillark, Sun Titan, Woe Strider's escape, Bolas's Citadel (top-of-library casting for life and the ten-permanent drain), Yawgmoth, Priest of Forgotten Gods, Warren Soultrader, High Market, Phyrexian Altar and Deadly Dispute. ## Card database by set

Cards are kept in a database filed by release set: one file per set in `goldfish/static/data/sets/` (`index.json` lists them all, [docs/SETS.md](docs/SETS.md) is the readable table). Each card's full record (rules text, types and the simulator's coverage report for it) is stored **once, in the set it was first printed in**. Every later set that reprints it lists only a small pointer (`name`, `set`, `cn`, `rarity` and `ref`, the set that holds the full record), so nothing is duplicated.

- **Add a set:** `node tools/fetch-sets.js <code> [<code> ...]` with Scryfall set codes, for example `node tools/fetch-sets.js cmm c21`. Reprinted cards are filed in the set of their first printing, which is created as a "partial" set (only those cards) until you fetch the whole set.
- **File the cards of your decks:** `node tools/fetch-sets.js --import-decks` adds every card of the decks in `tests/fixtures/*-loaded.json` under the set it was first printed in. The deck fixtures themselves are left as they are, because the tests read them.
- **The most played cards:** `node tools/fetch-sets.js --top 1000` files the 1,000 most played Commander cards (Scryfall carries EDHREC's popularity rank) and lists them in `goldfish/static/data/top-cards.json`. Rules work goes down that list from the most played card.
- **Any Scryfall search:** `node tools/fetch-sets.js --query "t:saga"` files every card a search returns under its first-printing set.
- **Re-check after engine work:** `node tools/fetch-sets.js --report` re-reads every full record with the current engine and rebuilds the index and the table.
- **Added so far:** Commander Masters, Commander Legends, Commander Legends: Battle for Baldur's Gate, Modern Horizons 2 and 3, Tales of Middle-earth Commander and Commander 2018 to 2021 as whole sets, plus the cards of all the decks in the tests under the sets they were first printed in. `tests/engine/sets.test.js` checks that every card is stored once, every pointer leads to a full record in an earlier set, and the simulator can read every card.

## Built-in decks

The deck menu on the Deck tab starts with a library of ready-made decks, grouped by color identity: five colors first, then four, three, two, one and colorless. Pick one and it loads straight into the simulator. The same decks are listed as opponents for the mirror match, so any deck can play any other.

The lists live in `goldfish/static/decks/` as plain text (one deck per file; the first line is `// Deck name (Commander)`, and every line that is not a card, such as the deck name, notes and section markers, starts with `//` so it is never read as a card). To add or change a deck, edit or add a `.txt` file there and run `node tools/build-deck-index.js` (it looks up each commander's color identity on Scryfall and rewrites `decks/index.json`). `node tests/run.js` checks that every list parses and that the index matches the files.

## Saved decks

Tap **Manage** on the Deck tab to rename, duplicate, copy, export or delete saved decks, and to back them all up to one file (**Export all**) or bring them back (**Import**). Decks live in your browser only, so export a backup before clearing browser data or switching devices.

## Tests

`node tests/run.js` runs the engine scenario tests offline (about 250 checks); `node tests/run.js --ui` runs the browser tests. See `tests/README.md`. Add a test file for every card or rule you add to the engine.
