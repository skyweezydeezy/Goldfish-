## Reddit post (r/EDH)
**Title:** I made a free goldfish simulator for Commander decks. Paste a list and see how it really runs (feedback wanted)

**Body:**
> Hi all. I built ICEYfish, a free browser tool for goldfishing Commander decks. No sign-up, no ads, and it works on phones.
>
> You paste a decklist, and the tabs are:
> - **Build**: curve, tags and the deck's strengths
> - **Mulligan**: opening hands with London mulligans and exact land odds
> - **Practice**: play the deck by hand against a goldfish, or autopilot turns, with undo
> - **Simulate**: 2,000 autopiloted games by default: mana by turn, land drops, commander cast turn, kill turn
> - **Odds**: the exact chance of drawing the cards you need by each turn
> - **Duel** and **Pod**: play against computer opponents (one, or a four-player pod), or **Play a friend** on another device (beta)
>
> Card data and images come from Scryfall. The rules engine doesn't know every card yet, so I'd love to hear about cards it gets wrong, or anything that's confusing.
>
> https://skyweezydeezy.github.io/ICEYfish/
