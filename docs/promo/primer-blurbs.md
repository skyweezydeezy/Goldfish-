# Primer blurbs: Moxfield and Archidekt

Drop one of these at the end of a deck primer or description. Link: https://skyweezydeezy.github.io/ICEYfish/
Both sites render Markdown links; the QR code (`goldfish-qr.png`) can be uploaded as an image where images are allowed (e.g. a Moxfield primer/Imgur link), but a plain link is safer.

Rules of thumb: say it's free and needs no sign-up, make it about the deck (not an ad), and don't repost the same text across many decks in a row.

## 1. One-liner (description / tagline)
> Want to see how this deck actually runs? Paste the list into [ICEYfish](https://skyweezydeezy.github.io/ICEYfish/), a free goldfish simulator with opening hands, mana-by-turn charts and draw odds.

## 2. Short (end of primer)
> **Goldfish it yourself**
> Curious how consistent the mana is? Paste this decklist into [ICEYfish](https://skyweezydeezy.github.io/ICEYfish/). It's free, has no sign-up, and works on your phone. Try the **Mulligan** tab for London-mulligan sample hands and **Simulate** for mana by turn and when the commander usually lands.

## 3. Medium (deck-tech section)
> ### Testing the list
> I tuned the land count and curve with [ICEYfish](https://skyweezydeezy.github.io/ICEYfish/), a free browser goldfish tool (no account needed). Paste the list and you get:
> - **Mulligan**: fanned opening hands with London mulligans and exact land-count odds
> - **Practice**: play the deck by hand against a goldfish, or autopilot the turns, with undo
> - **Simulate**: thousands of autopiloted games: mana by turn, land-drop odds, commander cast turn, goldfish kill turn
> - **Odds**: the exact chance of seeing at least *k* of any group of cards by each turn
> - **Duel** and **Pod**: play against computer opponents (one, or a four-player Commander pod), or **Play a friend** on another device
>
> If you find something in my list the sim gets wrong, tell me in the comments.
